Turn stream highlights into TikTok clips

Streamfan finds the best moments of long-form videos and streams, edits them into short vertical clips with captions, and publishes them to TikTok through TikTok's own official tools.

How it works

1

Find the moments

Long-form video is analysed to pick the highlights that match a campaign's brief.

2

Edit the clips

Each highlight is cut into a vertical short with captions, ready to publish.

3

Publish to TikTok

The account owner connects with TikTok Login Kit, and clips are sent with the Content Posting API.

What Streamfan is for

Streamfan is a publishing tool for creators and clip channels. It does not have its own accounts, passwords or social features: you sign in on TikTok's own page, and Streamfan only receives the access that TikTok grants after you approve it.

  • Publishing short clips to the TikTok account that connected, and only to that account.
  • Using the clips the account owner chose to produce — nothing is posted without that account being connected first.
  • Respecting TikTok's visibility rules: until an app is audited by TikTok, posts stay private to the creator.

Your data and permissions

Streamfan asks TikTok for the minimum it needs. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

TikTok product / scopeWhy Streamfan uses it
Login Kit · user.info.basicIdentifies the TikTok account that connects (its open ID and public profile name).
Content Posting API · video.publishSends the produced clip to the connected account so it can be published there.

Streamfan never sees your TikTok password, and you can remove its access at any time from your TikTok app permissions.
